Comparative judging

A second, independent method per track. Judges choose the stronger of two projects they have already reviewed, so each choice is informed and a judge's leniency cancels inside the comparison. Choices are fitted with Bradley–Terry and shown beside the calibrated rubric order. This is advisory: it never changes the official ranking. The only place pairwise evidence enters the ranking is a tie-break round, as pre-registered. Each judge is asked for {{ target }} comparisons per track, and a track starts once a judge has reviewed {{ min_pool }} of its projects.
