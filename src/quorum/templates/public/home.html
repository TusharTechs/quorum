Self-hosted · offline · open source

Every project judged. Every tie decided. Every team answered.

Quorum runs a hackathon from registration to published results. It keeps every project at quorum across an asynchronous judging window. It spends spare judge time only where a prize is still in doubt, and settles statistical ties with a pre-registered pairwise round. Then it sends every team its scorecard, and shows its maths on every result.

Quorum

Assignment designed so judge harshness is measurable. Reminders, a completion forecast, and one-click reassignment when a batch stalls.

Focus

After the baseline, extra reviews go only to projects whose prize is still uncertain. The same judge hours make better decisions.

Verdict

Calibrated scores with an exact per-judge explanation, uncertainty, and a pre-registered tie-break. The method is hashed before registration opens.

Answer

A feedback guarantee, then a scorecard for every team, placed or not. Judges get signed, numbered evaluation protocols.
